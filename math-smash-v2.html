Axiom Guide

Objective: Break Their Proof

Unlike traditional health-bar fighters, Math Smash is a platform brawler. Landing hits builds your rival's Damage %—the higher their percentage, the farther every hit launches them.

  • Launch rivals beyond the left, right, top, or bottom blast zones to score a K.O.
  • Each fighter starts with 3 Stocks and a 3:00 match clock.
  • If time expires, the fighter with more stocks (then lower %, then more KOs) wins.

Movement & Platforms

  • Double Jump: Press W / ↑ twice to recover back onto the stage.
  • Drop-Through: Press S / ↓ while standing on upper blue platforms to drop beneath them.
  • Fast-Fall: Hold S / ↓ while descending to snap back down faster.
  • Phase Dash: Press Shift / E (or double-tap A/D) for a brief invulnerable burst through projectiles.

Q.E.D. Meter & Ultimates

Both dealing and taking damage charge your golden Q.E.D. bar beneath your special meter.

  • At 100%, press Q (P1) or U (P2) or gamepad Y to unleash your mathematician's signature theorem finisher.
  • Opening activation grants brief invulnerability and a freeze-frame impact flash.

Theorem Drops (Optional Mutator)

Enable Theorem Drops in the pre-match bar to spawn glowing mathematical pickups on platforms:

  • π (Irrational Speed): +30% movement speed for 6 seconds.
  • Σ (Empowered): Instantly resets special cooldown, grants +25% Q.E.D., and boosts your next hit by 1.5×.
  • ∅ (Null Set): Erases all active enemy projectiles, Gravity Wells, and Euler orbits.

Directional Melee & Clashing

Your basic attack (F / J) changes arc depending on direction held:

  • Neutral / Side Swing: Fast horizontal cleave for spacing and edge pressure.
  • Up + Attack: Vertical anti-air arc that juggles airborne rivals upward.
  • Air + Down + Attack: Meteor Spike—slams aerial opponents downward for early bottom blast-zone KOs.
  • Projectile Clash: Active melee swings destroy light projectiles (Pythagoras shards, Ada punch cards) and bat Mirzakhani's billiard ball back!

P1 & P2 Keyboard Controls

  • Move: P1 AD · P2 ←→ (solo accepts either)
  • Jump ×2 / Drop: P1 W/S · P2 ↑ (Space)/↓
  • Attack / Special: P1 F/G · P2 J/K
  • Phase Dash / Q.E.D.: P1 Shift(E)/Q · P2 L/U
  • Shortcuts: 1–0 quick-pick, Esc/P pause, H guide

Gamepad & Touch

  • Move / Drop: Left Stick or D-Pad
  • Jump / Attack / Special: A / X / B
  • Phase Dash / Q.E.D.: LB or RB / Y
  • Pause: Start (auto-detects 2nd pad in 2P Duel)
  • Touch Screens: Coarse-pointer devices auto-show the touch pad, or toggle Touch in the footer.

Quick Match Tips

  • Use Up + Attack when rivals try to land on top of you from upper platforms.
  • Bait Emmy Noether's golden Q ward before committing heavy hits—or dash through projectiles instead of eating them.
  • Against Newton, jump or Phase Dash away from his Gravity Well before its final detonation.

Four Arenas

  • Axiom Arena: Balanced tournament layout with one solid main stage and three pass-through platforms.
  • Infinite Hotel: Side elevator platforms oscillate smoothly up and down, carrying grounded fighters.
  • Bell Curve: Wide main floor with a raised central arch and staggered side ledges for vertical battles.
  • Möbius Strip: Horizontal screen edges wrap seamlessly from left to right—KOs happen via top/bottom blast zones!

Game Modes & Mutators

  • 1 Player: Battle a random rival at Casual, Standard, or Ruthless AI difficulty.
  • 2 Players: Local shared-keyboard or dual-gamepad versus match (includes mirror-match outline).
  • Gauntlet: 4-round escalating arcade ladder across all 4 arenas with best-time persistence.
  • Lemma Lab: Infinite practice mode with damage reset, dummy behavior (Stand/Jump/Evade), and live hitbox & velocity vector overlays.
